VoxReach

(AI Cold-Calling Voice Agent Platform)

AI voice agents that call your leads, qualify them and book meetings. Every call is transcribed, every result is recorded, and the calling rules are enforced on every dial.

See it in under three minutes

Build an agent, rehearse it in the browser, import leads, start the dialer and open a finished call. The campaign runs in simulation mode with fictional leads; no real calls were placed.

From a script to booked meetings

Everything a sales team or agency needs to run outbound calls with AI, in one place.

Voice agents

  • Built from your script: goal, pitch, qualifying questions, objection handling and booking rules.
  • Rehearse in the browser by microphone or keyboard before any real call.
  • Your choice of AI model, in the cloud or on your own server, with a scripted engine built in.
  • Hand-off to a person when a prospect asks for one.

Campaigns

  • CSV lead import with consent and timezone per lead.
  • Pacing controls: calls at once and calls per minute.
  • Automatic retries for no answer, busy and voicemail.
  • Simulation mode to preview a campaign before paying for calls.

Every call on record

  • Transcript, result, summary and sentiment for each call.
  • Sales outcomes: meeting booked, interested, call-back, not interested.
  • CSV export and a webhook to your CRM.
  • Live dashboard of calls, connect rate and meetings.

Built for agencies

  • Client workspaces with separate agents, leads, calls and logins.
  • Team roles: owners manage settings, members run campaigns.

Your own phone number

  • Live calls go out through your own phone account, so caller ID and call costs stay yours.
  • Choose the language and voice for each agent.

Simple to run

  • One small server with its own database.
  • Works on phones and desktops.
  • Passwords hashed, provider keys encrypted at rest.

Calling rules, enforced by the platform

Responsible calling is built in, not left to the AI model's judgement.

AI disclosure

Every call opens by saying it is an AI assistant and who it is calling for. It cannot be switched off.

Consent gate

Leads without recorded consent stay in the list but are never dialled.

Calling hours

Permitted hours and days apply in each lead's own timezone, with a cap on attempts.

Do not call

Anyone who asks to stop is added to the list automatically and never called again.

The platform enforces these rules, but the caller remains responsible for following the telemarketing law of the places they call.
